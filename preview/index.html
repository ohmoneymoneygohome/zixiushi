03 — About · 關於

Three inks,
one study.三色，一室

The palette is pulled from the painting itself — the navy of a night wash, the cyan of a thin glaze, the pink of a mineral ear.

顏色取自畫裡：夜洗的藍、薄罩的石青、礦物耳尖的粉。

I don't paint Chinese painting. I don't paint Western painting. I work in the gap between them.

我不畫國畫，也不畫西畫。我畫的是兩者之間的那道縫。

Paper, ink, mineral colour — I know these, and I know the rules. You learn the rules so you know where you stand when you leave them. I work alone, in one room: no assistant, no model, no audience.

紙、墨、礦物色，我熟；規矩我也懂。懂規矩，是為了離開它的時候知道自己站在哪裡。我一個人在一間屋子裡畫：沒有助手、沒有模特、沒有觀眾。

Works 001–021 were made in 2026 on two sizes of paper — people, fish, a wall, an ear, one afternoon of light. They are not a series. They are the same stretch of days.

〇〇一至〇二一作於二〇二六年，兩種紙幅——人、魚、一面牆、一隻耳朵、一個下午的光。它們不是系列，是同一段日子。
